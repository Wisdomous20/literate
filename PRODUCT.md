# Product

## Register

product

## Users

LiteRate is used by teachers, school administrators, and reading program staff who need to assess student reading ability, organize classes, and review progress without adding manual scoring overhead.

## Product Purpose

LiteRate helps educators run oral reading, fluency, and comprehension assessments with audio capture, AI transcription, miscue analysis, and student/class reporting. Success means educators can move from assessment setup to trustworthy results with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, instructional. The product should feel clear enough for busy teachers, credible enough for assessment data, and warm enough for an education setting.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, childish education visuals, clinical admin software, heavy dark overlays, and overly decorative auth screens that distract from signing in.

## Design Principles

Keep the task obvious. Authentication and assessment workflows should make the next action immediately clear.

Use warmth with restraint. Brand color and illustration should support the experience without overwhelming form controls or data.

Build trust through clarity. Labels, validation, and states should be plain, accessible, and consistent.

Reference the landing page. Auth and public surfaces should share the same clean white base, purple accent, soft illustrated background, and grounded button vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, labeled form controls, clear error recovery, and motion that respects reduced-motion preferences.
