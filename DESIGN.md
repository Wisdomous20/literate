# Design

## Design System

LiteRate uses a clean, light product interface derived from the landing page. The visual language is warm educational software: white surfaces, soft lavender atmosphere, rounded rectangles, and purple accents used for action and navigation.

## Color

- Background: soft white and very pale lavender washes.
- Primary text: `#323743`.
- Secondary text: `#575E6B`.
- Primary accent: `#6C4EEB`.
- Accent support: `#B3A4F1`, `#F3F0FF`, `#EDE8FF`.
- Secondary accent: `#FF5DA2`, used sparingly on marketing CTAs.
- Error: red text and pale red surfaces with clear contrast.

## Typography

Use Poppins for product and marketing surfaces. Headings are bold with compact line height. Body copy stays short, readable, and direct.

## Components

Buttons use rounded rectangles, clear focus states, and the landing page's grounded purple shadow only when emphasis is needed. Inputs should be simple white controls with lavender borders and visible focus rings. Avoid thick pseudo-3D input borders on forms.

## Layout

Auth pages should be centered, spacious, and responsive. Desktop can use a two-column panel with illustration on one side and the form on the other. Mobile should prioritize the form and avoid hiding essential navigation.

## Motion

Use short 150-250ms transitions for hover, focus, and active states. Avoid decorative page-load choreography on product surfaces.
